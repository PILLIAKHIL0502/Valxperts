Common questions

Before you reach out.

The things people usually ask us first. Nothing is off limits.

Do you work on-site or remote?
Both. We travel for site visits, kickoffs, inspections, and whenever the real work is on the shop floor. The rest runs on your preferred collaboration stack. Our usual mix is a week on site at the start, then alternating weeks as the project needs.
How quickly can you start?
Scoped assessments can usually start within a week. Named-lead engagements go within three weeks, sometimes sooner if a lead is between projects. For emergency audit support we have a 48-hour commitment.
What is your pricing model?
Fixed fee for discrete deliverables (a scoped assessment, a single-system qualification, a gap report). Time and materials for programs where scope evolves with findings. You see a weekly burn report with hours by workstream either way, and a change-control process for anything that moves scope.
Do you sign NDAs before the first call?
Yes. We are happy to work from your template, or we can send ours. Either way, nothing proprietary leaves your systems without written agreement from you.
Can you work alongside our existing validation partner?
Yes. We regularly augment internal teams and other consultancies. Our deliverables fit any document system, and we are comfortable sharing credit, not just invoices.
What is your minimum engagement size?
A two-day scoped assessment is the smallest thing we take on. Below that, we would rather point you to a free resource than take your money and under-deliver.

Ready to make a service engagement concrete?

Bring us the system or the deadline. We will scope it in two business days, name the lead, and show you what the first week looks like.

Before you write

A few things worth knowing.

What should I include in my first message?
The system or process you would want help with, the deadline or trigger (new project, audit, inspection), and whether you have validation partners already. Three sentences is enough. If a diagram would help, attach it; we can read it.
Do I need to sign an NDA before you will talk?
No. Most first conversations happen without one. If you would rather have one in place, send us your template and we will have it signed before the call.
How long until I hear back?
Usually one business day. Always within two. If more than two business days pass without a reply, something has gone wrong on our end and we would want you to tell us.
Can we skip the form and just hop on a call?
Yes. Email us at contact@valxperts.com with a two-sentence context and a window that works for you. We will put a practice lead on the call. Twenty minutes, no deck.
Will I speak with a sales rep?
No. Every first conversation is with a practice lead. If we think we are not the right fit for your problem, we will say so and point you to someone who is.
